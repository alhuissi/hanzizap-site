HanziZap Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026

HanziZap does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information or learning activity.

Information stored on your device

Your answers, learning progress, preferences, scores, and unlocked content are stored locally on your device. This information is used only to provide the app’s features. You can erase it from Privacy & Help inside the app, or by deleting the app.

Accounts, tracking, and third parties

HanziZap has no user accounts, advertising, analytics, tracking technologies, or third-party data-collection SDKs. The app does not use your microphone and does not record your voice.

Audio

Pronunciation uses speech synthesis provided on your device. Learning text is not sent by HanziZap to an external service.

Children

HanziZap does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes

If the app’s data practices change, this policy will be updated before the changed version is released.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the HanziZap support page.
