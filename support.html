HanziZap Support

HanziZap is an offline Mandarin-learning app for iPhone.

Audio troubleshooting

Open Arcade Club → Audio to test sound effects and Mandarin speech. Check the iPhone volume, Silent Mode, and Bluetooth audio destination. Pronunciation also requires a compatible Mandarin voice installed on the device.

Resetting progress

Open Privacy & Help inside HanziZap and choose Reset Saved Learning Progress. The app asks for confirmation before erasing local progress.

Report a problem

Open a support issue on GitHub. Include your iPhone model, iOS version, HanziZap version, and the steps that reproduce the problem. Do not include personal or sensitive information.
